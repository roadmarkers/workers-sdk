Update Astro template for v6 and `@astrojs/cloudflare` v13

The scaffolded project now uses the Cloudflare Vite plugin under the hood, which runs your Worker in the `workerd` runtime during development. See the [Astro Cloudflare adapter migration guide](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/integrations-guide/cloudflare/#upgrading-to-v13-and-astro-6) for details.
